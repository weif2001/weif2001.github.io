Hi! I’m Wei Fan, a fourth year PhD student in [Statistics and Data Science](https://statistics.wharton.upenn.edu/) at the Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania. I'm very fortunate to be advised by Prof. [Yuting Wei](https://yutingwei.github.io/).

My research interests include high dimensional statistics, reinforcement learning, bandits and online learning.

Previously, I was an undergraduate at the University of Science and Technology of China, where I received a Bachelor of Science (B.S.) in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics.

## News
 - \[2025/12\]We’re excited to share our [recent paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.00222) on the asymptotics of LinUCB, with [Kevin](https://hetankevin.github.io/) and Yuting. We show that even though LinUCB collects dependent data, its estimator still converges to a normal distribution asymptotically—just with a scaling of $T^{1/4}$ rate rather than the classical $\sqrt{T}$. This enables us to construct confidence intervals and perform valid statistical inference. Feel free to check out if you are interested!
